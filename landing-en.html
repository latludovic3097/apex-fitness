FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Why is the app free?
This is a personal project. No company behind it, no acquisition cost, no team to pay. Firebase hosting costs nothing up to 1 GB of storage. If the app ever reaches 10,000 active users, an optional donation or premium tier may follow. Never ads.
Is it actually safe for my back?
The app follows published protocols (McGill). But it is not a medical device. If you have a diagnosed condition, consult a physiotherapist before starting. The L4-L5-S1 mode reduces risk compared to a standard program — it does not eliminate it.
Is my data secure?
By default, everything stays on your device (localStorage). If you enable cloud sync (Google sign-in), your data is stored in Firestore with Security Rules that allow only you to access your document. No analytics, no sharing, no monetization.
Why not a native iOS / Android app?
FITStark is a PWA (Progressive Web App). Install it from your browser in 1 tap and it behaves like a native app (offline, full-screen). Benefits: one codebase, instant updates, no app store fees, works everywhere.
Can I contribute?
You can share the app with others, send feedback via the contact form, or support the project on Ko-fi. Every bit helps.
